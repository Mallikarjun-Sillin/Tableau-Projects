# ✈️ Airline Delay Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

This Tableau project analyzes airline flight delays across different airlines, airports, months, and days of the week. The dashboard helps identify delay patterns and provides insights into factors that may affect flight performance.

## Project Objectives

* Analyze flight delay trends across airlines and airports.
* Identify airlines with the highest delay rates.
* Compare airport performance based on delay percentages.
* Understand how delays vary by month and day of the week.
* Visualize flight activity geographically using an airport map.

## Dashboard Features

* Total Flights KPI
* On-Time Flights KPI
* Delayed Flights KPI
* Delay Rate KPI
* Airline Delay Rate Analysis
* Airport Delay Rate Analysis
* Monthly Delay Trend
* Day of Week Delay Analysis
* Interactive Airport Map
* Dynamic Filters for Airline, Airport, Month, and Day of Week

## Key Insights

* Some airlines experience significantly higher delay rates than others.
* Flight delays vary across airports, with certain airports showing consistently higher delay percentages.
* Delay rates fluctuate throughout the year, with some months experiencing more delays than others.
* Delays also vary by day of the week, indicating operational and scheduling patterns.
* Geographic visualization helps identify regions with higher flight delay activity.
